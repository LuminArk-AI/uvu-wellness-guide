UVU Student Mental Wellness Resource Guide

Hey Wolverines! College gets heavy sometimes. We pulled together the real, publicly listed support options on campus and around Utah County so you don't have to go digging when you need them.

Heads up: This guide just points you to campus and community supports. It's not clinical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you're in immediate danger, call 911. For mental health crisis support, call or text 988.

1. Crisis and Urgent Help

If you're thinking about hurting yourself or someone else, or you just need help right now, start here.

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

  • Call or text 988. It's free, confidential, and 24/7.
  • Prefer typing? You can chat online on the Lifeline website.
  • It's for any emotional or mental health struggle, not only suicide crises.
Source: 988.utah.gov and 988lifeline.org

SafeUT (app and web chat)

  • Free 24/7 chat with a licensed counselor for Utah students.
  • Grab it on the Apple App Store or Google Play, or start a chat at safeut.org.
  • You can also send confidential tips about safety concerns.
Source: safeut.org

Crisis Text Line

  • Text HOME to 741741 to reach a trained crisis counselor, 24/7.
  • Some UVU pages list texting START to 741-741 instead. Either keyword works per UVU's pages, or just call or text 988.
Source: UVU Crisis Services

Emergency and Campus Safety

  • Call 911 for immediate danger.
  • UVU Police: 801-863-5555 (or 5555 from a campus phone).
  • Head to the nearest hospital ER if you need in-person emergency care.
Source: UVU Crisis Services

2. UVU On-Campus Resources

Stuff you get as a UVU student. Use it!

Student Health Services: Mental Health

  • Where: Sorensen Center, SC 221.
  • SHS office hours: Mon/Wed/Fri 8am to 5pm, Tue/Thu 8am to 6pm.
  • Mental Health Services hours: Mon to Fri, 8am to 5pm.
  • Phone: 801-863-8876
  • Individual and couples therapy: $10 a session (enrollment rules apply, usually 9+ credits fall/spring, 6+ summer).
  • Group therapy: free with 1+ credit.
  • New individual clients schedule through the digital portal on the Mental Health Services page.
Source: UVU Mental Health Services

UVU Crisis Services (business hours)

  • Mon to Fri, 8:00am to 4:15pm (not during campus closures): call 801-863-8876 and ask to schedule with a UVU Crisis Therapist.
  • Crisis appointments are listed as free.
  • These are for urgent support, not ongoing therapy.
Source: UVU Crisis Services

TimelyCare (virtual, 24/7)

  • 24/7 virtual mental health and well-being support for UVU students.
  • UVU lists 6 free scheduled counseling sessions per year (Aug 1 to Jul 31). Email deanofstudents@uvu.edu to request a TimelyCare license.
  • TalkNow: log in at timelycare.com/support with your UVID email and password.
Source: UVU TimelyCare and UVU Student Health

"I'm Not Okay" and "Find Help" pages

  • UVU pages that match how you're feeling to campus, virtual, and community options.
  • Great if you're not sure where to start.
Source: I'm Not Okay and Find Help

CARE Hub (basic needs)

  • Help with food, housing, health, safety, childcare, transportation, and more, through referrals and case managers. Basic needs stress hits mental health hard, so this counts.
  • Where: SL 101 (outside entrance under the SLWC Bridge).
  • Hours: Mon to Fri, 8am to 5pm.
  • Phone: 801-863-2273, email care@uvu.edu
  • Also runs the UVU Food Pantry and Fresh Food Fridays.
Source: uvu.edu/studentcare

3. Off-Campus and Community Resources

For after hours, when campus is full, or if you'd rather go local.

Wasatch Behavioral Health

  • Public behavioral health center serving Utah County.
  • Phone: 801-373-4760
Source: wasatch.org

Wasatch Receiving & Outreach Center

  • Adult crisis walk-in: 24/7 short-term crisis evaluation and stabilization.
  • Phone: 801-852-2131
  • 1175 East 300 North, Provo, UT 84606
Source: Wasatch Crisis Services

2-1-1 United Way

  • Dial 2-1-1 or use the 211 app or website to find local health, housing, and support services.
  • Listed by UVU Student Health Services.
Source: UVU Student Health

The Trevor Project (LGBTQ young people)

  • Call 1-866-488-7386, or use chat and text on their site.
  • Listed on UVU Crisis Services as LGBTQ-friendly support.
Source: thetrevorproject.org and UVU Crisis Services

Trans Lifeline

  • Peer support run by and for trans people: 877-565-8860
  • Listed on UVU Crisis Services.
Source: UVU Crisis Services

4. Self-Care Tips

Everyday habits lots of students find helpful. They're not a replacement for real care.

  • Keep a simple routine for sleep, meals, and classes when life feels chaotic.
  • Take breaks from news and social media if scrolling makes stress worse.
  • Move a little every day. Walk across campus or stretch between classes.
  • Text or hang out with one trusted person instead of handling everything alone.
  • Try the free stuff first: the group therapy intro workshop (Mon 12 to 1pm, SC 221), TimelyCare TalkNow, and SafeUT chat.
  • If stress sticks around more than a few days or you feel hopeless, reach out early to SHS or 988.

5. How to Use This Guide

  1. Need help right now? Go to Section 1.
  2. Want ongoing support as a UVU student? Check Section 2 (SHS portal, crisis therapist during business hours, TimelyCare).
  3. Campus is full, it's after hours, or you'd rather go local? Try Section 3.
  4. Waiting on an appointment? Section 4 has everyday coping ideas.
  5. Share this page with friends, but leave the clinical advice to the pros.
